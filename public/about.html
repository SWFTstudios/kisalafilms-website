Who I am

Motorcycles. Automotive energy. Action sports. Adventure. Vinyl film and cinema — I’m chasing all of it, and connecting with people who feel the same wherever they come from.

Elombe Kisala

Kisala Films is a 2-in-1: film as vinyl wrap & paint protection film, and film as cinematic photos and videos. I’m based in Jersey City, wrapping bikes one set of hands at a time.

Right now I’m taking a limited number of founding builds — 6 of 8 still open — at the early rate, and documenting every one of them. I don’t have a catalog of client wraps or YouTube episodes yet. That’s the whole point of the founding slots: the portfolio gets built in public, on real bikes, with the camera running, instead of appearing fully formed one day.

What a founding rider gets is the early price and premium cast film, panels off, edges wrapped properly — the same work I’ll be charging more for once those slots are gone. What I get is the build in the reel. Feature on the YouTube channel or this site is optional — only if you want it. Prices stay transparent either way.
