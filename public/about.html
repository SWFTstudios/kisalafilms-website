Who we are

One rider, one garage

K Films is a motorcycle and helmet wrap shop in Jersey City. Not a chain, not a franchise, and not an automotive shop that also does bikes.

Jersey City, NJ

Elombe Kisala

I ride, and I wrap bikes one set of hands at a time. Motorcycles are the whole business — not a sideline to a car shop.

Right now I’m taking a limited number of founding builds — 6 of 8 still open — at the early rate. I don’t have a catalog of client wraps or YouTube episodes yet. That’s the whole point of the founding slots: the portfolio gets built in public, on real bikes, instead of appearing fully formed one day.

What a founding rider gets is the early price and premium cast film, panels off, edges wrapped properly — the same work I’ll be charging more for once those slots are gone.
