Our process

Easy. Simple. Seamless.

Four steps from the first message to the day you ride out. Nothing starts until you have seen the plan and the price.

  1. 01

    Step 1 — Consult

    Send the bike, the helmet and the idea. We talk through what suits the bodywork, what the film can and cannot do, and where the budget lands.

  2. 02

    Step 2 — Design

    Pick the colour, the finish and the coverage. Full change, accents, a livery, or a helmet built to match the bike.

  3. 03

    Step 3 — Install

    Panels come off, surfaces are cleaned and the film goes on. Edges wrapped, not trimmed flush.

  4. 04

    Step 4 — Enjoy

    You get the bike back with care instructions, and something nobody else on your ride is running.

Getting the bike here

Ride it in, or we come to you

Drop-off

Ride or trailer the bike to the Jersey City garage by appointment. No charge.

Pickup

We collect the bike and bring it back to the garage. Pickup starts at $75, and pickup with return delivery afterwards starts at $150. Both are quoted with the build rather than charged up front.

Timelines

You get a timeline in writing once the bike has been looked at. A bike that needs bodywork addressed first takes longer, and you will hear that before any film is cut.
