The stages

Six steps,all visible.

Every stage shows in your client portal as it happens, with photos from the bench and every quote, payment and receipt in one place.

  • 01

    Start your build

    Year, make and model, photos of the bike and what you have in mind, sent online in a few minutes. No payment needed.

  • 02

    Your quote

    A written quote off your photos, with the film, the work and any transport each on their own line. Questions answered in the same reply.

  • 03

    Reserve your date

    Approve the quote in your client portal and pay a deposit online to hold your studio date.

  • 04

    Drop-off or pickup

    Ride it in by appointment, or have it collected from Brooklyn or NYC.

  • 05

    In the studio

    Inspection, strip-down, wrap, rebuild and a final check under raking light. Progress photos land in your portal at each stage.

  • 06

    Ride out

    Pay the balance online, collect the bike or have it delivered, and get a care guide for your film.

Aftercare

Look after it,it looks after you.

  • 01

    Give it a week

    Let the film settle for about a week before the first wash.

  • 02

    Hand wash

    Two buckets and a soft mitt. Keep pressure washers well away from edges.

  • 03

    Right products

    No abrasive polish, and matte-safe products only on matte and satin film.

  • 04

    Store it covered

    Sun is what ages film fastest. A garage or a breathable cover adds years.
