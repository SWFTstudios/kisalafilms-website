Fraunces + Hanken Grotesk is the production pairing — both are free, open-source (Google Fonts), and load via the link tag above. No licensing required.

Display / Headline

Fraunces

Google Fonts · Undercase Type

An expressive variable serif with soft, old-style curves and a wet-ink feel at large sizes. Reads editorial and cinematic without the over-used flatness of Playfair Display. Use the italic optical size for the most character.

Free · variable font · OFL license

Paid alternatives if you outgrow it: GT Sectra Display (Grilli Type) · Canela (Commercial Type)

Body / UI

Hanken Grotesk

Google Fonts · Marcus Sterz

A clean, slightly warm grotesque with good screen legibility at small sizes — quiet enough to let Fraunces carry the brand's personality.

Free · variable font · OFL license

Paid alternatives if you outgrow it: Söhne (Klim) · National 2 (Klim)

This pairing costs nothing and can ship today. Revisit the paid alternatives only if you want an extra edge over other NYC studios once the brand is established.

03

Buttons & CTAs

Book a Shoot Outline gold — primary navigation CTA
Watch Reel Solid gold — single highest-priority action
View Portfolio Ghost / muted — tertiary link

Outline gold for primary navigation CTAs, solid gold reserved for the single highest-priority action on a page (usually one per screen), ghost/muted for tertiary links.

04

Imagery Treatment

Warm shadow grade, soft vignette, low-key lighting

Grade toward warm shadows and cool-neutral highlights. Keep blacks slightly lifted rather than crushed so skin tones stay readable in dark environments. Avoid bright, evenly-lit "catalog" lighting — every hero image should feel like a still from a film, not a stock photo.
