Jersey City, NJ, specifically

You can just ride it over

Downtown, Journal Square, the Heights, Greenville, West Side — nowhere in Jersey City is a long ride to the garage. Drop-off is by appointment and costs nothing, and we lock the window once the quote is agreed so your bike isn’t sitting around waiting on me.

Or don't, and I'll come to you

Plenty of riders here keep the bike in a shared garage, a driveway they share with three neighbours, or on the street under a cover. If moving it is the annoying part, pickup starts at $75 and I’ll handle it.

Winter is the honest time to do this

A wrap needs the bike off the road for a couple of days, and warm panels take film better than cold ones. If you’re laying the bike up over a Jersey winter anyway, that’s the cheapest downtime you’ll ever spend.
