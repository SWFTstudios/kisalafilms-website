K Films — est. Jersey City

A motorcycle is read in a glance. Every edge, seam and finish should hold up to a second look.

Panels come off. Film goes around the edges, not just up to them. Seams sit where the factory lines already are. It takes longer, and it is the reason the bike still looks right a few seasons from now.

How a build runs

01 / 03

Protect

Self-healing paint protection film over the tank, fairings and every surface a boot, buckle or stone will find. Invisible in gloss, or turned to satin.

02 / 03

Transform

Process

From your phone to the open road.

Send the bike’s details and photos online. From then on, every quote, payment and progress photo lives in your client portal.

  1. 01

    Start your build

    Year, make and model, a few photos and what you have in mind. Five minutes, from anywhere.

  2. 02

    Quote & reserve

    A written quote off your photos. Approve it and secure your studio date with a deposit online.

  3. 03

    In the studio

    Panels off, film on. Progress photos land in your portal at each stage, so you see it happen.

  4. 04

    Ride out

    Final inspection, care guide and the balance paid online. Collect it, or have it delivered back.
