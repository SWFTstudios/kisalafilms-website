Film & care

Quality cast film protects factory paint when it is installed and removed correctly. The risk is with paint that is already compromised: oxidised, badly resprayed or lifting. Panels in that state are discussed before anything is wrapped.

Cast films are generally rated for several years, and how long yours lasts depends on sun, storage and washing. A garaged bike ages far slower than one outside all summer.

Paint protection film: a clear, thicker, self-healing film over the areas that take physical damage. On a motorcycle that usually means the tank, knee areas and leading edges. It can go over paint or over a wrap.

Wait about a week after collection, then hand wash with a soft mitt. Keep pressure washers away from edges, avoid abrasive polish, and use matte-safe products on matte and satin film.
