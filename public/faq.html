Quality cast vinyl protects factory paint when it is installed and removed correctly. The risk is with paint that is already compromised — oxidised, previously resprayed badly, or lifting — because removal can take that with it. We will not wrap panels in that state without talking about it first.

Cast films from the manufacturers we buy from are generally rated for several years on vertical surfaces, and how long yours actually lasts depends on sun, storage and washing. A bike kept outside all summer ages faster than one in a garage. We’ll tell you what to expect for the specific film you pick rather than quote a headline number.

Paint protection film — a clear, thicker film that goes over the areas that take physical damage rather than changing how the bike looks. On a motorcycle that usually means the tank where your knees and jacket buckle sit, and the leading edges that catch stones. It can go over paint or over a colour-change wrap.

Light scratches, yes. Vinyl is thin and it follows what is underneath, so deep scratches, dents and flaking clear coat will still read through the film — and a lifting edge will make the wrap fail early. Deeper damage needs sorting before the film goes on. Send photos with your quote and you’ll get a straight answer.
