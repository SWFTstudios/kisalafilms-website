System

Style guide

The v3 design system: near-black canvas, one red accent, Barlow Condensed over Inter. Everything on the site is built from what is on this page, and nothing here is a picture of the tokens — the swatches and samples are rendered by the same variables the pages use.

Colour

Palette

Dark surfaces separate by a hair, not by contrast — the photography does the work. Red is the only chromatic colour in the system and it marks one thing per screen: the action you want taken.

Tokens
