How pricing works

Prices start where shown. The real number depends on your bike: how much bodywork comes off, the film you choose and the condition of the paint underneath.

Send the bike's details and photos and you get a written quote, with transport as its own line. Approve it and reserve your studio date with a deposit online; the balance is paid when you collect.
