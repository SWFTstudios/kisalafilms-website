Home / The Studio

One workshop.One pair of hands.

K Films is a motorcycle film studio in Jersey City. Not a chain, not a franchise, and not a car shop that also does bikes.

Elombe Kisala, founder

I ride, and I wrap bikes one at a time. Motorcycles are the whole business, and every one that comes through the studio gets the same slow, panels-off treatment.

The portfolio is being built in public, on real bikes, rather than appearing fully formed one day. The first riders through the door get the founding rate for it, and the same film and finish I'll charge more for once those slots are gone.
