Home / Finishes

Choose afinish.

Hundreds of colours, eight kinds of surface. Start with the finish; the colour is easier to choose once you know how you want the bike to catch the light.

Gloss

Deep, wet reflections that read like fresh paint.

Best for a factory look, or a colour change that should pass for paint.

Satin

A soft sheen that follows the bodywork's lines without mirroring everything around it.

Best for modern sport and naked bikes. Hides minor imperfections well.

Matte

Flat and dense, with no reflection at all.

Best for stealth builds. Needs matte-safe care products.

Metallic

Fine metal flake under a clear layer that catches light as the bike moves.

Best for warm colours: bronze, copper, champagne.

Colour-shift

Changes colour with the angle you see it from.

Best on curvy bodywork where the shift has room to move.

Chrome

A true mirror finish, the most demanding film to install.

Best used as an accent rather than on a whole bike.

Carbon & textured

Woven carbon, brushed metal and other textures you can feel.

Best on tank tops, tails and trim.

Clear protection

Invisible urethane film over paint or over a wrap, in gloss or satin.

Best on every bike, in the places that wear first.
