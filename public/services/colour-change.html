Service 01

A colour change should look like the bike left the factory that way. That means no visible seams across a panel, no paint showing at the edges, and no film trimmed flush where it should have been wrapped.

What's included

Done properly,every time.

  • 01

    Panels off

    Fairings, tank covers and tail come off so film wraps the edges instead of stopping at them.

  • 02

    Premium cast film

    Conformable cast film that holds compound curves without shrinking back.

  • 03

    Edges and gaps wrapped

    Film turned around every edge and tucked into panel gaps, where a flush trim would lift.

  • 04

    Seams on factory lines

    Where a join is unavoidable, it sits on a line the bike already has.

  • 05

    Surface prep

    Decontamination wash, clay and an alcohol wipe before any film touches a panel.

  • 06

    Reassembly and QC

    Every panel refitted, every edge checked under raking light before handover.

How it's done

Four stages,no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    Paint, plastics and any old film are checked first. Problems surface before the quote is final, not on collection day.

  2. 02

    Strip down

    Bodywork comes off and is laid out panel by panel.

  3. 03

    Wrap

    Each panel is wrapped on its own, heated and post-heated so the film sets into its shape.

  4. 04

    Rebuild

    Panels go back on, fixings torqued, gaps and edges checked again in place.
