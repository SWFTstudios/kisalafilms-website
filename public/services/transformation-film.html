The idea

The name is not only about vinyl

K Films started with a camera as much as a squeegee. A wrap takes days and most of that is invisible once the panels go back on, so it gets shot while it happens.

What gets covered
The bike as it arrives, the strip-down, film going on, and the finished machine once it is back together.
What you get
Stills you can use, and a short edit for social when the build warrants one.
Going public
Entirely optional. Plenty of bikes never leave the folder, and that costs you nothing either way.
Photo or video only
Shoots without a wrap go through Roller Reels rather than here.
