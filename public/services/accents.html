Not every bike needs a full change. A satin tank on a gloss bike, a single scratched fairing covered, a contrast tail: done with the same panels-off care, partials can say more than a whole new colour.

What's included

Done properly,every time.

  • 01

    Single panels

    Cover a scratched or faded panel without changing the whole bike.

  • 02

    Two-tone splits

    A second colour or finish split along the bodywork's own lines.

  • 03

    Tank and tail

    The two panels that change a bike's character most.

  • 04

    Contrast finishes

    Satin over gloss, gloss over matte, or a metallic flash.

  • 05

    Panels off

    Same as a full wrap: edges wrapped, not trimmed.

  • 06

    Matched to the bike

    Film chosen to sit alongside the factory colour, not fight it.

How it's done

Four stages,no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Plan the split

    Which panels, which finish, and where one meets the other.

  2. 02

    Remove

    Only the panels being wrapped come off.

  3. 03

    Wrap

    Each panel wrapped edge to edge and post-heated.

  4. 04

    Refit

    Panels back on and checked against the rest of the bike.
