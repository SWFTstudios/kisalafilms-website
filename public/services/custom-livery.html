Service 04

A livery is designed for one bike. Lines follow the bodywork, numbers sit where they can be read, and every panel is laid out before printing so the design meets itself across the gaps.

What's included

Done properly,every time.

  • 01

    Design session

    Your references and ideas turned into a first concept.

  • 02

    Panel-by-panel layout

    The design mapped onto your bike's actual panels, so lines carry across gaps.

  • 03

    Written proof

    You approve the layout before anything is printed.

  • 04

    Printed and laminated

    Printed cast film with a protective laminate.

  • 05

    Panels off

    Installed with the same edge-wrapped method as a colour change.

  • 06

    Files kept

    Your design is kept on file for repairs or a matching helmet later.

How it's done

Four stages,no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Brief

    References, colours, numbers and anything you want on the bike.

  2. 02

    Design and proof

    A layout on your bike's panels, revised with you until it is right.

  3. 03

    Print

    Printed and laminated once the proof is signed off.

  4. 04

    Install

    Panels off, livery on, rebuilt and checked.
