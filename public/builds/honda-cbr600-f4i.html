The brief

A fully faired bike is the hardest shape to wrap well: long panels, deep curves and a lot of edges. Exactly the reason to make it the first.

Every fairing came off. Film was wrapped around each edge and into the gaps, seams were kept to the lines the bodywork already has, and the bike was rebuilt and checked panel by panel before it was shot.
